# A Quantitative Analysis to Evaluate and Enhance Operational Efficiency and Profitability in a Pharmaceutical Retail Store

A comprehensive business analytics project that analyzes sales, purchase, and inventory data of a pharmaceutical retail store to identify opportunities for improving operational efficiency, inventory management, and profitability.

## Overview

This project was completed as part of the IIT Madras BS Degree Business Data Management Capstone.

The objective was to perform quantitative analysis on real-world business data collected from a pharmaceutical retailer and provide data-driven recommendations for improving business performance.

The analysis covers:

- Sales analysis
- Purchase analysis
- Inventory analysis
- Product profitability
- Customer demand trends
- Inventory efficiency
- Business recommendations

---

## Business Problem

The retail store faced several operational challenges:

- Inefficient inventory management
- Dead stock accumulation
- Lack of demand forecasting
- Difficulty identifying high-profit products
- Poor pricing decisions
- Limited visibility into business performance

The project aimed to convert raw business data into actionable insights that could improve operational efficiency and profitability.

---

## Dataset

The project uses three datasets collected from an operational pharmaceutical retail store.

### Sales Data

- 5,020 records
- 14 features

Includes:

- Product Name
- Manufacturing Company
- Quantity
- MRP
- Discount
- Grand Total
- Payment Mode

### Purchase Data

- 905 records
- 11 features

Includes:

- Product Name
- Quantity Purchased
- Purchase Rate
- Discount
- Supplier Cost

### Inventory Data

- 5,719 records
- 18 features

Includes:

- Product Name
- Current Stock
- Balance
- MRP
- Purchase Rate
- Category
- Tax Information

## Project Workflow

### Data Collection

- Collected three months of sales and purchase records
- Gathered inventory data from store software
- Consolidated multiple datasets into structured CSV files

### Data Cleaning

- Handled missing categorical values using mode imputation
- Corrected data types
- Removed duplicates
- Standardized dataset structure

### Exploratory Data Analysis

Performed analysis including:

- Descriptive statistics
- Sales distribution
- Monthly sales trends
- Product-wise sales
- Product profitability
- Manufacturer performance
- Inventory analysis
- Outlier detection

### Business Analysis

Generated insights on:

- Top-selling products
- Most profitable products
- High-demand manufacturers
- Dead stock
- Seasonal demand patterns
- Sales vs profitability relationship

---

## Key Findings

- Identified top-selling and most profitable products separately, showing that high sales do not always result in high profit.
- Found significant pricing and profit outliers affecting overall profitability.
- Detected approximately 27% unsold inventory, indicating inefficient stock management.
- Identified seasonal sales variations between October, November, and December.
- Highlighted leading manufacturers contributing the highest revenue.
